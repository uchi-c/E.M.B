What we do

Ten services. One accountable team.

From the first cut into the ground to the day a unit is lifted onto a truck for its next site — we manage every stage of the modular building lifecycle in house.

The full service line

Every stage of the modular building lifecycle, delivered by one accountable Evaregi team.

01

Prefabricated buildings

Steel-framed, sandwich-panelled modular buildings engineered for durability, comfort and flexibility across every sector we serve.

02

Modular offices

From a single site office to a fully functional multi-storey HQ — open-plan floors, meeting rooms, reception and canteen space.

03

Site preparation

Clearing, levelling, foundations, plinths, drainage and concrete slabs — the ground is ready before a single panel arrives.

04

Civil works

Concrete foundations, hardstands, access roads and drainage infrastructure engineered to support the structures above them.

05

Electrical & plumbing

Wiring, lighting, air-conditioning, water and drainage systems — fully operational the moment the keys are handed over.

06

Turnkey projects

Planning, procurement, logistics, installation and commissioning — one point of contact, from concept to final handover.

07

Relocation services

Safe dismantling, transport and reinstallation as your site or scale requirements change — buildings that move with you.

08

Maintenance

Structural repairs, panel and component replacement, refurbishment and preventative maintenance to extend the life of every unit.

09

Rental solutions

Short or long-term rental terms — ideal for construction sites, mining operations, temporary offices, events and project accommodation.

10

Purchase solutions

Competitive pricing for clients who want to own their units outright, with a full turnkey project delivered after purchase.

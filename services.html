What we do

Five services. One accountable team.

From the first cut into the ground to the day a unit is lifted onto a truck for its next site — we manage every stage of the modular building lifecycle in house.

01 · SITE PREPARATION

Civil works, before a single panel arrives.

At the client's request, we ensure every site is properly prepared to support the prefabricated structures before installation begins.

  • Site clearing and levelling
  • Concrete foundations and plinths
  • Drainage solutions
  • Concrete slabs
02 · MECHANICAL, ELECTRICAL & PLUMBING

Fully operational, from day one.

We install all necessary building services — electrical wiring, air-conditioning, water and drainage systems — so the prefab unit is fully operational the moment it's handed over.

Electrical wiring & lighting
Air-conditioning
Water & drainage
03 · MODULAR MAINTENANCE

Extending the working life of every unit.

Our modular buildings are durable and low-maintenance by design — built for harsh African site and remote-camp conditions, with spares and support readily available.

  • Structural repairs
  • Replacement of panels and components
  • Refurbishment and upgrades
  • Preventative maintenance
04 · TURNKEY PROJECTS

One point of contact, start to finish.

We provide complete project delivery, managing every aspect of installation from planning to completion.

  • Project planning and coordination
  • Procurement and logistics
  • Installation and commissioning
  • Final handover
05 · RELOCATIONS

Move and grow with your buildings.

The key advantage of a modular building is mobility. Our buildings can be expanded, relocated, stacked or reconfigured as your operational needs change — we handle safe dismantling, transport and reinstallation for existing units.

We also scale buildings up or down to match your changing requirements.

We aim to partner with you

On time, within budget, to a high standard.

Sectorial space solutions

One system, six ways to work.

We deliver fast, reliable and cost-effective infrastructure for construction projects, mining operations, government institutions and private clients — the same modular unit, configured for how each sector actually operates on the ground.

Mining

Built for the pace and terrain of a mine site.

Mining operations need site offices, workshops and workforce accommodation on the ground before production can start — often in remote, difficult-to-reach terrain. Our modular units are transported, installed and commissioned fast, and engineered to hold up to demanding, dusty, high-traffic conditions.

Available on short or long-term rental for the life of the operation, or for outright purchase where the site is permanent.

  • Site offices, control rooms and stores
  • Workforce accommodation and ablutions
  • Rapid deployment to remote, hard-access sites
DURABLE · LOW MAINTENANCE

Scales as the operation does

As exploration turns to production — or a pit reaches end of life — units can be expanded, reconfigured, relocated or scaled down with the operation, protecting your capital investment through every phase.

Construction

Site infrastructure that moves at the project's pace.

Every construction project needs site offices, stores and crew welfare facilities up before the main works start, and cleared away cleanly when the project closes out. We supply, install and later relocate that infrastructure, so contractors can focus on the build.

Rental terms are matched to the programme — short-term for a single phase, or long-term across a multi-year contract.

  • Site offices, meeting rooms and stores
  • Ablutions, canteens and crew welfare units
  • Fast strike-down and relocation between phases or sites
FAST TO MOBILISE

One contractor, full accountability

As an NCC Grade 6, Category B registered contractor, we manage site preparation, installation and building services in house — a single point of accountability for your site infrastructure.

Government

Accountable delivery for public-sector projects.

We deliver modular administrative offices, satellite facilities and community infrastructure for government institutions — procured through a fully registered, compliant Zambian contractor with current PACRA, ZRA and NCC credentials on file.

Fixed specifications, clear costing and a documented handover process support institutional procurement requirements from tender through to commissioning.

  • Administrative and satellite offices
  • Community and institutional facilities
  • Documented compliance for public procurement
REGISTERED & COMPLIANT

Verifiable credentials, on request

Certificate of Incorporation, tax registration, tax clearance and NCC contractor registration are all available to view and download on our certifications page.

Education

Classrooms, ready in weeks.

We are a leading innovator in Alternative Building Technology in Zambia. Our prefabricated classrooms are the fastest option for additional space at existing schools, and for turnkey new-school projects — quick to set up, with a high-quality finish.

Build quality is the cornerstone: our classroom concepts use natural light and convection-based ventilation, with thermal insulation to moderate temperatures in extreme conditions.

FAST & COST EFFECTIVE

Self-contained, and built to grow with the school

Cabin classrooms are manufactured in a factory-controlled environment and transported to site on a flatbed truck — self-contained, with insulated wall and roof panels, vinyl floors and aluminium windows, comfortably accommodating 30–40 children at a time.

  • Quick installation
  • Safe and comfortable learning environment
  • Expandable layouts as enrolment grows

Commercial offices

From an on-site sales office to a multi-storey HQ.

Modular buildings are frequently used as temporary or permanent office space. We offer a full range of designs, from a single site office through to fully functional multi-storey office blocks — up to three storeys, on a sturdy steel frame.

Interiors are designed around your requirements: open-plan space, management offices, meeting and board rooms, reception areas and canteen space.

SITE BUILD

A faster route to a permanent building

We also provide panelised buildings, factory-fabricated and erected on site on a reinforced concrete raft foundation. The floor plan is totally flexible and tailored to your exact needs — a faster, more cost-effective alternative to traditional brick building. As a permanent structure, a site build is not relocatable.

Accommodation camps

Camps built for remote, on-site workforces.

We build and install accommodation camps as a total solution for staff accommodation needs — developed to meet the lifestyle expectations of on-site contracted workforces in remote areas.

A full turnkey camp includes different levels of accommodation, ablutions, kitchens, laundries, security and recreational facilities — giving you peace of mind to focus on your core business.

CAMP MAINTENANCE

Reconfigure or relocate as the camp changes

The modular nature of our units allows for maximum flexibility and up to a 3-storey build. Each unit is delivered totally self-contained, and as your camp's needs change the units can easily be reconfigured or relocated.

Our buildings are durable and low-maintenance, designed for harsh African remote-camp conditions — spares and support are readily available.

Who we are

Fast, cost-effective space — without the conventional build timeline.

Evaregi Building & Maintenance Co. Ltd is a Zambian construction company specialising in the design, supply, installation and relocation of prefabricated modular buildings, for businesses, institutions and project sites across the country.

Our units are engineered for durability, comfort and flexibility — ideal for organisations that need rapid deployment and infrastructure that can adapt as operations change.

Our products

One module. Almost any building.

Every Evaregi building starts from the same standard 3m × 6m steel-framed, sandwich-panelled unit — combined, stacked up to three storeys, and customised with partitions, wiring, plumbing and HVAC to fit the job.

  • Insulated wall panels, wiring & lighting fitted as standard
  • Windows, doors and air-conditioning compatibility
  • Available to rent short or long-term, or purchase outright

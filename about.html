About Evaregi

Space is temporary. The way we build it isn't.

Evaregi Building & Maintenance (EBM) is a construction company specialising in the design, supply, installation and relocation of prefabricated modular buildings — incorporated in Lusaka in August 2020.

Who we are

Built to meet a growing demand for flexible space.

We provide fast, cost-effective and high-quality buildings as space solutions for businesses, institutions and project sites. Our modular structures are designed to meet the growing demand for flexible, scalable and rapidly deployable buildings across multiple sectors.

With a strong focus on quality, speed of delivery and customer satisfaction, we help organisations create functional space without the long timelines associated with conventional construction.

Our mission

Innovative, durable, cost-effective — every time.

"To deliver innovative, durable, and cost-effective prefab building solutions that meet the evolving needs of our clients."

  • Fast delivery and installation
  • Competitive pricing
  • Flexible rental and purchase options
  • Professional installation team
  • Reliable after-sales support

Our vision

Zambia's most trusted name in modular space.

"To be the leading provider of prefabricated and modular building solutions in Zambia — recognised for the quality, speed and reliability of every space we deliver."

Why modular buildings

The advantages that make modular the right choice for businesses, institutions and project sites.

Speed to occupy

Units are manufactured off site while civil works proceed in parallel, cutting overall programme time.

Cost control

Fixed, factory-controlled specification keeps costs predictable against conventional construction.

Mobility

Expand, stack, relocate or scale down units as your operational needs change.

Built to last

Steel frame and sandwich-panel construction, engineered for harsh African site conditions.
